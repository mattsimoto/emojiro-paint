Emojiro Paint

A playful paint, animation, and music toy built around Unicode emoji.

Made for phones

Large touch targets, swipe-safe drawing, horizontally scrollable music staff, and local saves.

15 synthesized instruments

The composer uses emoji as musical stamps while Web Audio generates the sound in the browser.

Portable projects

Artwork lives in the browser, songs export as JSON, and paintings export as PNG files.

Original presentation

Emojiro Paint recreates the creative-workstation idea with original interface code and audio synthesis rather than Nintendo art or sound assets.
