Emojiro Paint

A playful paint, animation, and music toy built around Unicode emoji.

Made for phones

Large touch targets, swipe-safe drawing, horizontally scrollable music grid, and local saves.

No samples required

The music engine synthesizes its sounds in the browser with Web Audio, so the app can remain lightweight.

Portable projects

Artwork lives in the browser, music can be exported as JSON, and paintings can be exported as PNG files.

Original presentation

Emojiro Paint borrows the spirit of classic console creativity software without using Nintendo art or audio assets.
